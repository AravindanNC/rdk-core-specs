    <h1>Core RDK Entertainment Platform</h1>

RDK8 advances app infrastructure through Firebolt Connect, supporting standardized, scalable delivery for web and native apps across RDK-powered devices.</p>

<section class="section">
  <div class="eyebrow">RDKE overview</div>
  <h2>Why choose RDK-E?</h2>
  <p class="lede">RDK-E standardizes core platform functionality while allowing operators, OEMs, and technology partners to focus on creating differentiated user experiences and services. The platform provides a layered architecture that improves portability, maintainability, and time-to-market.</p>
  <div class="grid">
    <article class="card"><h3>Open and reusable</h3><p>An open, modular platform built from reusable components that can be configured across STB entertainment platforms.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Consistent interfaces</h3><p>Common APIs and services that help applications work consistently across diverse device platforms.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Hardware portability</h3><p>Layered abstractions that reduce hardware coupling and simplify integration across SoCs and vendor platforms.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Faster delivery</h3><p>Reusable platform capabilities that help operators, OEMs, and technology partners focus on differentiated experiences.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section alt">
  <div class="eyebrow">Architecture</div>
  <h2>RDKE architecture</h2>
  <div class="grid architecture-grid">
    <article class="card"><h3>Application Layer</h3><p>Firebolt apps, the Lightning reference UI and the Firebolt framework, where portable third-party and OTT streaming apps run.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Middleware Layer</h3><p>A single, consistent implementation of core device functionality: the Thunder framework, entertainment services, media and DRM, and device management.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Vendor Layer</h3><p>BSP, device drivers, HAL implementations, SoC adaptations and Linux OS integration, abstracting hardware differences behind the vendor layer.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="eyebrow">Current release</div>
  <h2>RDK8 Release Overview</h2>
  <p class="lede">Released as a Development Preview, RDK8 enables early validation of key capabilities, accelerates ecosystem readiness, and reduces integration risk. It also provides a clear signal of platform direction, helping operators and partners plan product roadmaps and investments with greater confidence.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="eyebrow">RDK8 benefits</div>
  <h2>Key benefits of RDK8</h2>
  <div class="grid benefit-cards">
    <article class="card"><h3>Early validation of next-generation app delivery</h3><p>Gives developers and partners an opportunity to validate standardized app onboarding and integration through Firebolt Connect, including support for new App Managers and container-based technologies such as DAC 2.0, while reducing late-stage risk.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Accelerated app lifecycle readiness</h3><p>Enables earlier validation of app lifecycle behavior through Firebolt Connect, helping ensure alignment with W3C standards, Firebolt 8.0, and more consistent app behavior across devices.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Faster ecosystem enablement and feedback</h3><p>Allows developers, OEMs, and silicon partners to test on real implementations sooner, gather practical feedback, and reduce fragmentation by enabling vendors in parallel rather than sequentially.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Lower risk for operators</h3><p>Helps operators assess new capabilities early without impacting live deployments, supporting more confident planning and adoption.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Faster app time to market at scale</h3><p>Standardized app onboarding and validation through Firebolt Connect streamline optimization and support repeatable deployment across the RDK ecosystem.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Early performance and stability insights</h3><p>Gives developers visibility into CPU, memory, GPU, and I/O performance on real hardware before field deployment.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Lower long-term platform costs and smoother user experience</h3><p>Improved performance and power efficiency, driven in part by Vulkan adoption, help reduce long-term costs while enhancing the user experience.</p></article>
    <article class="card"><h3>Revamped OSS Model</h3><p>Enables flexible, platform-specific OSS integration, improving adaptability and driving greater community reuse and contributions.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
